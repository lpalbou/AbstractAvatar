# Acknowledgements

AbstractAvatar is part of AbstractFramework, created by Laurent-Philippe Albou.

The procedural catalog draws on the AbstractAssistant Apple Watch and iPhone artwork and the
AbstractTUI shaded activity avatars. Those implementations provide the design vocabulary and
provenance described in the [catalog](docs/catalog.md).

WebAssembly supplies the portable browser execution format. Python, Rust and platform UI toolkits
provide the integration surfaces. No machine-learning model is needed to render the catalog.
